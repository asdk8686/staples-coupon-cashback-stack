Staples discounts come out of four separate pots, and they don't all play nicely together. Two of them stack cleanly. Two of them can cancel each other out depending on which code you use. Once you know which is which, a routine paper-and-ink order stops being a guessing game.

Here's how the layers actually behave, what the current offers look like, and how to place an order so that everything that should track, tracks.

On a $300 office chair haul, that's $9 you traded for a $10-off code — roughly a wash, except you also spent ten minutes hunting the code.

The practical rule is blunt: **check the offer page before you paste a code, not after.** If a code isn't listed where you clicked through, assume the cashback is gone or at risk, and decide whether the code is worth more than 3%.

## Easy Rewards, in plain numbers

Easy Rewards is Staples' free loyalty program, and the mechanics are refreshingly simple once you stop trying to find hidden tiers:

- **1 point per $1** on qualifying purchases in US stores, on staples.com, in the app, by phone, and through select third-party marketplaces.
- **200 points = $1** when redeemed at checkout. That's a 0.5% base return — modest.
- **Up to 3 bonus categories** you choose yourself, earning double points or more. This is the actual lever. If you buy copy paper monthly, making paper a bonus category doubles the earning rate on your biggest recurring line item.
- Points redeem in whole-dollar increments, and they don't expire as long as your account stays active — earning or redeeming at least one point within an 18-month window keeps it alive.

The catch that trips people up: **offers must be activated before checkout.** If you forget, you don't earn the associated points, and there's no retroactive fix. Staples advises checking the dashboard regularly, which is accurate — the personalized offers rotate and several are time-boxed.

Gift cards, phone cards, postage stamps, clearance items, taxes, shipping, protection plans, direct mail services and promotional products don't earn points at all. Points from in-store purchases or shipped online orders usually land within 24–48 hours, though Staples says it can take up to 30 business days.

## How to place a Staples order so it actually tracks

Cashback failures are almost never billing disputes. They're missed steps. The Staples offer page is unusually explicit about a few of them, which is useful:

1. **Start a fresh cart.** The terms require you to clear your Staples cart, close the Staples app, then open the cashback platform and click through to Staples from there — and to do this for every purchase. A cart left over from a previous session is a common reason a purchase never shows up.
2. **Complete the order in one go.** Don't hop tabs, don't check email mid-checkout, don't switch browsers. If the visit gets interrupted by an app-update or download screen, restart from the platform.
3. **Accept the store's cookies.** Staples can only confirm a transaction to the cashback platform if you accept the cookie banners on its pages. Declining them can mean the order never gets verified.
4. **Turn off ad blockers and don't use a VPN.** Both can break the tracking link. Clicking through from a Facebook ad, a Google ad or another browser extension on the way to checkout can also overwrite the session.
5. **If payment fails, restart from the platform.** A payment error mid-order is a common moment for tracking to drop, so re-enter through the offer page rather than hitting refresh on Staples.

That's five rules and none of them cost money. The cart-reset rule is the one people skip most often, because it feels pointless — the terms say it's required "for each purchase," so treat it as part of the routine. Clear cart, close app, start from the platform, buy.

## The quieter savings: shipping and AutoRestock

Coupon hunting gets all the attention, but two structural things save more money over a year than codes do.

Free next-day delivery currently applies to most items once your order clears the **$35 threshold**, weekdays only. Ink and toner orders above the same threshold ship free too. ## Quick answers to the questions that come up most

**Does a Staples coupon always cancel my cashback?** No. Codes featured on the cashback platform are fine. The risk is third-party codes scraped from aggregators — those are the ones most likely to void it.

**Should I use a code or take the cashback?** Do the math on the specific order. A $15-off-$60 code on a $60 basket beats 3% ($1.80). A $5-off code on a $300 furniture order doesn't beat $9.

**Can I combine Easy Rewards points with a coupon?** It depends on the coupon's terms — Staples states the conditions differ per promotion, so read the offer before assuming. Points redemption and a promo code on the same transaction frequently don't stack.

**Why did my order show $0 cashback?** Check the exclusions first (laptop, ink, toner, gift cards), then check whether a code was applied, then check the cart-reset and cookie requirements.
